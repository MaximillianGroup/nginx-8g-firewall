# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and professional environment.

Examples of positive behavior:

- Respectful feedback
- Assuming good intent
- Focus on production-quality outcomes
- Clear, evidence-based technical discussion

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or abusive language
- Sharing private/security-sensitive information publicly

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers.
